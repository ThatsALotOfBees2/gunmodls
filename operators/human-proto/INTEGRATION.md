# Human operator prototype: integration guide

For anyone (or any Claude session with no context) who rebuilds, changes or ships this character.
Units are metres and degrees. "Blender space" is Z up, the character faces -Y, his left is +X. glTF space is Y up,
he faces +Z, his left is +X. glTFast negates X on import (Unity +X = glTF -X); he faces +Z in Unity too.

## 1. Where the source is and how to rebuild

Everything is generated by scripts; nothing is hand-edited in Blender. A copy of every script is in `source/`
(`source/build/*.py|js|mjs`, `source/viewer/app.js`, `template.html`, `style.css`). Pick a working folder `$H`, copy
`source/build` to `$H/build` and `source/viewer` to `$H/viewer`, and set up the environment below. (The session that
made it worked in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/54ae899d-ab9c-5ac8-991a-a7fa61aa138b/scratchpad/human`, a scratch folder
that may be gone; its `head/textures/` and `gear/textures/` bakes are reproduced by `--retex` / `--rebake`.)

Environment:
- Blender 5.x as a Python module in a venv: `python -m venv $H/venv && $H/venv/bin/pip install bpy numpy pillow`.
  Headless Cycles on CPU works; Workbench and EEVEE do not (no EGL).
- MakeHuman 1.x source with its data: `git clone https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman $H/mh`. The scripts
  read `$H/mh/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`, `targets/`, `rigs/default.mhskel`, `rigs/default_weights.mhw` and
  `eyes/`. MakeHuman's base mesh, targets and rigs are CC0.
- Node in `$H`: `npm install gltf-validator gltfpack @gltf-transform/core @gltf-transform/extensions
  @gltf-transform/functions meshoptimizer esbuild three@0.180.0`. Headless checks use Playwright from
  `/home/user/gunmodls/tools/node_modules`.

One command builds the whole character from the MakeHuman data (about 2.5 min; add `--rebake` after changing gear
geometry, `--retex` after changing `head_tex.py`, `--renders` for the Cycles shots):

    $H/venv/bin/python $H/build/build_all.py --renders --deliver /home/user/gunmodls/operators/human-proto

Stages (each reads only the previous stage's `.blend`, each runs on its own too):

| # | Script | Does | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `mh_body.py` | parses `base.obj` itself (never Blender's importer: it reorders vertices), applies the macro and detail targets, builds the rig from the `.mhskel` joint vertex lists, skins with `default_weights.mhw`, renames bones for Unity Humanoid | `base/human_base.blend` |
| 2 | `head_tex.py` | numpy-bakes the skin (albedo with pores, stubble, freckles, capillaries; normal; ORM), hair strand, eye textures on the MakeHuman UVs | `head/textures/` |
| 3 | `head_build.py` | skin / eye / cornea materials, 860 brow and 320 lash strands, `HairShell1..5` | `final/stage/human_head.blend` |
| 4 | `gear_build.py` (+ `gear_boots.py`, `gear_finish.py`) | shirt, trousers, gloves (offset layers of the body with its weights), boots, pads, plate carrier, bakes their textures, deletes the body faces under clothing | `final/stage/human_gear.blend`, `gear/textures/` |
| 5 | `integrate.py` + `anim.py` | game textures (JPEG), realtime material fixes, the 5 clips, the collar re-weight, the poke-through pass, LOD1/LOD2, the three GLB exports | `final/human_proto.blend`, `final/human-proto.glb`, `final/human-proto_lods.glb`, `final/report.json` |

| 7 | `pack_web.mjs`, `make_viewer.js`, `viewer_check.js` | meshopt compression (names kept), bundles `viewer/app.js` with three r0.180.0 and embeds the GLB; headless swiftshader load, console errors, screenshots | `final/viewer.html`, `final/checks/viewer/` |
| 8 | `posecheck.py`, `hero_render.py` | Cycles pose sheet of every clip; delivery renders | `final/checks/poses/`, `final/renders/` |

`report.json` lists triangles per mesh and LOD, the clips and the poke-through counts.

## 2. Changing the face and body

All shape settings are at the top of `mh_body.py` (`MACRO`, `DETAIL`; the bone map is `HUMANOID`):
- **Macro sliders** (blended exactly as MakeHuman's `apps/human.py`): gender 1.0, age 0.5385 (30 years; 0.5 = 25,
  1.0 = 90), muscle 0.82, weight 0.58, height 0.54 (= 1.79 m; very sensitive: 0.62 gives 1.91 m), proportions 0.85,
  race 0.80 / 0.12 / 0.08 (caucasian / african / asian).
- **Detail targets**: the `DETAIL` list of (`targets/<group>/<name>.target`, weight) entries (about 50: head square, chin, brow,
  cheekbones, nose hump, lips, eyelids, neck, torso V-shape, limb muscle, ears). Add any of MakeHuman's 1500+
  targets the same way (`ls $H/mh/makehuman/data/targets/*/`). Some target files are empty; the loader handles it.
- Skin tone, stubble, freckles and the hair fade are in `head_tex.py` (re-run with `--retex`).

The rig joints follow the morphs, the clothing is rebuilt from the morphed body, so every downstream stage adapts.
Re-bake gear (`--rebake`) whenever the body shape changes, because the gear UV islands come from a smart projection of
the new geometry.

## 3. Swapping gear

Gear is in `gear_build.py`: region masks per body vertex (`m_shirt`, `m_trous`, `m_glove`, `m_boot`; the crew collar
height is `collar_z(y, x)`, which rises away from the neck so the cut follows the trapezius), offset layers
(`build_layer`), pads (`dome_pad`, `ellipse_patch`), plates (`plate`), cummerbund (`ring_band`), straps (`strap`) and
pouches (`box`). Each part is assigned to one of the joined export objects: `Uniform` (Clothing), `Boots`, `Pads`
(Polymer), `CarrierSoft` (Cordura) and `CarrierHard` (Polymer, Metal, LED). Garment vertices copy the weights of the
body vertex they came from; rigid parts use inverse-distance weights from the 6 nearest body vertices (plates
near-rigid on Chest/spine02/UpperChest). Body faces under clothing are deleted (`hidden_body_faces.json`), so a new
garment must cover its region or the body shows through holes.

To remove a part, drop it from its join list; to add a helmet, build it like the plates and give it 100 % `Head`
weight. Keep to the house style: dark alloy / grey-black, at most one or two small `#8a4dff` LEDs, no text or logos.

`integrate.py` then runs a poke-through pass over 21 frames of the clips: shirt vertices inside the carrier or pads
are pushed in (max 5 mm, never within 2.5 cm of a hem), skin under the shirt sinks 3 mm away from hems, and skin faces
fully covered by the shirt are deleted. The shirt's collar zone takes the skin's weights so the neckline holds in
every pose.

## 4. Rig and bone map

MakeHuman default skeleton, 163 bones, at most 4 influences per vertex, normalised. Rest pose: MakeHuman's A-pose
(arms about 42° down, elbows bent about 45° forward). Bone rolls are MakeHuman's (local X is the joint's bend axis).

| Unity human bone | File bone |
| --- | --- |
| Hips / Spine / Chest / UpperChest | `Hips` / `Spine` / `Chest` / `UpperChest` (`spine04`, `spine02` sit between them, unmapped) |
| Neck / Head / Jaw / eyes | `Neck` (`neck02`, `neck03` unmapped) / `Head` / `Jaw` / `LeftEye`, `RightEye` |
| Shoulder, UpperArm, LowerArm, Hand | `LeftShoulder`, `LeftUpperArm`, `LeftLowerArm`, `LeftHand` (and Right) |
| UpperLeg, LowerLeg, Foot, Toes | `LeftUpperLeg`, `LeftLowerLeg`, `LeftFoot`, `LeftToes` |
| Fingers | `LeftThumbProximal` ... `LeftLittleDistal` (Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, Little × Proximal, Intermediate, Distal) |

Unmapped extras: twist bones `LeftUpperArmTwist`, `LeftLowerArmTwist`, `LeftUpperLegTwist`, `LeftLowerLegTwist` (the
clips drive the forearm twist bone with 60 % of the hand's pronation), `pelvis.L/R`, `shoulder01.L/R`,
`metacarpal1-4`, the other toes, `breast`, tongue and the MakeHuman face bones (unused).

## 5. Clips

All clips loop, 30 fps, in place (no root motion; the root stays at the origin). They are baked from `anim.py`, which
computes every frame with its own FK plus an analytic two-bone IK (knee and elbow hinge planes aligned to the bones'
MakeHuman bend axis), so the feet stay planted and the hands go where they are told.

| Clip | Length | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Idle` | 4 s | breathing (chest, shoulders), one slow weight shift, small head drift, relaxed hands |
| `Ready` | 3 s | low ready: stock in the right shoulder pocket, muzzle 24° down, bladed stance (left foot forward), right index finger straight along the receiver |
| `Walk` | 1.1 s | heel strike, roll, toe off; hips bob, sway and counter-rotate; arm swing. Move the root at 0.94 m/s |
| `CrouchIdle` | 4 s | hips 0.30 m lower, right heel up on the ball of the foot, rifle hold |
| `LookAround` | 6 s | ready stance; turns 55° left, holds, 50° right, holds, back; the eyes lead the head by 0.18 s |

**Attaching a rifle.** In `Ready` at t = 0 the stock butt sits at glTF (-0.188, 1.339, 0.130) and the bore points
along glTF (0.091, -0.592, 0.801) (Unity: negate X). The right wrist is at (-0.176, 1.131, 0.243), the left at
(-0.073, 1.056, 0.412). Parent the weapon to `RightHand` with the offset that puts its butt and bore there in that
frame, or drive both hands with IK to the weapon's grip and handguard.

## 6. Unity setup (glTFast, Unity 2021.3 LTS to Unity 6)

1. Install glTFast (`com.unity.cloud.gltfast`, or `com.atteneder.gltfast` on 2021). Copy `unity/human-proto.glb`
   into `Assets/`. In the importer set **Animation: Legacy** (the clips play through an `Animation` component).
   For meshopt or KTX files, none are used here: the game GLBs are plain glTF 2.0 with JPEG/PNG textures.
2. Drag the model into a scene and add `HumanOperatorProto` (from `unity/Runtime/HumanOperatorProto.cs`) to its root.
   On Awake it builds a `LODGroup` from the `*_LOD0/1/2` meshes (switch heights 0.45 / 0.18 / 0.015, editable),
   sets the LED's emissive strength (6×; glTFast drops `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`), makes the hair shells,
   brows and lashes double-sided alpha-clip, puts the cornea in the transparent queue without depth writes, keeps
   thin strands from casting shadows, and plays `Idle`. `Play("Walk")` crossfades; `ForceLod(i)` forces a LOD.
3. Humanoid retargeting: select the imported model and run **Tools > Gunmodls > Human Proto > Build Humanoid Avatar**
   (`unity/Editor/HumanOperatorProtoAvatar.cs`). It maps the bones by name and records a T-pose (arms and fingers
   straightened along ±X on a temporary copy), saves `<model>_Avatar.asset` and, on a scene object, adds an Animator.
   His own clips stay Legacy; use the Avatar for Mecanim humanoid clips.
4. Materials: glTFast's shaders (or URP/HDRP Lit) render skin as plain PBR. For hero shots swap `Skin` to a
   subsurface skin shader using `skin_basecolor`, `skin_normal` and `skin_orm` (R = AO, G = roughness) from
   `textures/`. The cornea has no transmission; it is a clear alpha shell with opacity 0.12.
5. Colliders and hitboxes are not included; add capsules on Head, Chest, the limbs.

Budget: 68.5k / 27.6k / 11.7k triangles, 18 / 14 / 12 material slots, one skeleton. Textures: 2048² for skin,
clothing, carrier fabric and hair, 1024² for boots and eyes, about 6 MB of JPEG/PNG in total.

## 7. Viewer contract

`viewer.html` exposes `window.__model = { id: 'human-proto', ready, preview(), exportGLB(), debug }`. `exportGLB()`
returns the embedded (meshopt-compressed) GLB as base64; `preview()` hides the UI and frames the Ready pose.
`debug` has `set(clip, t)`, `clips()`, `lod(0|1|2|'auto')`, `wire(on)`, `lights('studio'|'overcast'|'night')`,
`spin(on)`, `goView('full'|'face'|'armour'|'back')`, `stop()`, `renderOnce()`, `stats()`, plus `camera`, `controls`,
`scene`, `renderer`. Realtime material tuning lives in `viewer/app.js` `tuneMaterials` (skin gets a light sheen, hair
alpha-to-coverage, cornea transparent, LED not tone-mapped).
