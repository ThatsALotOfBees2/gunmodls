# Haneul Tower: integration notes

These notes are for someone (or another Claude session) picking up the map with no context. They cover the
files, the coordinate frame, the node naming and extras, the surface types and gadget sizes, the
`layout.json` schema, the Unity setup, and how to edit the layout and rebuild.

The map is a Siege-style 5v5 attack/defend level. It covers three floors (F1 96F, F2 97F, F3 98F) of a fictional
supertall in Seoul in 2111, with 4 objective site pairs, attackers spawning outside, and a destructible interior.
`README.md` has the player-facing overview, and `DESIGN.md` has the full level design (callouts, routes, timings,
sightlines, construction rules).

## 2. Units and axes

- **Units and axes:** metres, +Y up. In plan, x points east and z points south, so north is −z.
- **Floor heights:** F1 is at y 0, F2 at 4.2 and F3 at 8.4. Floor to floor is 4.2 m, the ceiling is 3.1 m and
  the slab 0.3 m (it spans y−0.3 to y). Every hard and reinforceable wall rises to the slab underside
  (y1 = 3.9), so the 0.8 m plenum above the ceiling is closed room by room; interior glass stops at 3.1 m under a
  hard bulkhead `HARD_<wallId>_bulk` (3.1–3.9 m). Nothing sees or shoots through the plenum.
- **Plate:** the playable plate is x, z ∈ [−26, 26].
- **glTFast negates X on import:** glTF +X becomes Unity −X. Node transforms are converted for you. Any position
  read from `layout.json` or from extras needs x → −x (`HaneulTowerMap.FromLayout`).
- **Directions:** these survive the conversion as local axes, so use `transform.forward` and `transform.up`
  rather than world vectors from the data.

## 3. Scene hierarchy

```
haneul_tower (scene; extras hold the whole naming table, surface types, legend, sites, reinforce rules)
├─ F1, F2, F3      <F>_STRUCT  <F>_FACADE  <F>_EXTERIOR  <F>_PROPS  <F>_DECO  <F>_SLOTS
├─ EXTERIOR        EXTERIOR_STRUCT (terrace, ledges, bridges, neighbours)  EXTERIOR_DECO  EXTERIOR_ENV  EXTERIOR_SLOTS
├─ ROOF            99F mechanical floor, the tower above, the crown
├─ BACKDROP        BACKDROP_city (the whole city skyline: one mesh, one material) and BACKDROP_sky (no gameplay)
├─ MARKERS         spawns, objectives, cover, callouts, prop markers, reveal zones
└─ COLLIDERS       COL_ box empties
```

**The backdrop** is two nodes straight under `BACKDROP`, nothing else:

- `BACKDROP_city`: every piece of city skyline scenery (towers and apartment slabs, roofs, streets and their light
  strips, the river with its sheen and light streaks, embankments, expressways, the six bridges, the far city bands,
  the ridges, the hill and its broadcast tower, the holo screens on the hero towers, the beacons) as **one mesh with
  one material**, `backdrop_city`: 132k triangles in one primitive, always drawn as **one draw call**. There are no
  culling tiles in either glb. Its textures are one baked atlas set: baseColor 4096² JPEG (sRGB), emissive 4096² JPEG
  (sRGB, the material's `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` is 3.2) and metallic-roughness 2048² JPEG (G roughness,
  B metalness). There is no normal map (the backdrop is 100 m to 8 km away).
- `BACKDROP_sky`: the sky dome (8.8 km) with the cloud deck baked into its emissive texture (one mesh, one material).

The neighbour buildings that carry gameplay stay separate and typed (`HARD_NB_*` roofs, decks, hall, baffle and crane,
`BRIDGE_*`, `WALK_*`, the `ENV_NB_*` bodies under them and Haneul's own `ENV_tower_above`, `ENV_mech_band` and
`ENV_bmu_rail`), as do the East Tower holo screens (`DECO_X_exterior_holo_*`), because they are seen at arm's length.
The soft transparent atmosphere layers (ridge mist, rain haze rings, the halo behind the broadcast tower) are
viewer-only now, like the rain.

There are three animation clips:

- `cradle_cycle` (24 s loop) moves `BRIDGE_CR_cradle` between y 4.2 and 8.4.
- `shutters_close` and `shutters_open` (2.0 s each, eased) drive the `closed` morph target of all 166 `SHUTTER_`
  nodes from 0 to 1 and back. They are optional: `HaneulTowerMap.SetShutters` drives the same blend shapes per
  shutter, floor, room or site (§6a). Do not play a shutter clip and call the methods at the same time.

## 4. Node naming and extras

Every gameplay node name starts with a prefix that gives its surface type, because glTFast does not import
extras by default. Every typed node also carries glTF `extras` with at least `{surface, id, floor}` plus the
fields listed below. The scene's extras repeat this table (`naming`) and the `surface_types`.

| Prefix | Node | Key extras |

## 5. Surface types and Wall Fortification sizes

| Type (`surface`) | Prefix | Destructible | Reinforceable | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hard | `HARD_` | no | no | core, columns and column wraps, shear and stone walls, slabs, spandrels, structure |
| soft | `SOFT_` | yes | no | not used for walls (user rule: every breakable wall is reinforceable); soft props only |
| reinforceable | `REINF_` | yes | yes | the only breakable wall type: panel 0–3.1 m (floor to ceiling) sized for Wall Fortification widths, between hard junction ends; hard only in the plenum above the ceiling |
| glass | `GLASSI_` | yes | no | interior glazing |
| balustrade | `RAIL_` | no | no | 1.1 m glass rail: shoot and vault over |
| hatch | `HATCH_` | yes | yes | 1.2 m floor hatch; `wall-fortification-hatch` fits it |
| soft_floor | `SOFTFLOOR_` | yes | no | floor/ceiling patch for vertical shots |
| door_barricadable / window_barricadable | `BARR_D_` / `BARR_W_` | barricade | no | openings that take a barricade |
| opening | `OPEN_` | no | no | never barricaded |
| glass_breakable / glass_tempered | `GLASS_B_` / `GLASS_T_` | yes / no | no | curtain-wall lites |
| glass_floor | `GLASS_TF_` | no | no | walkable tempered floor |
| holo | `HOLO_` | emitter only | no | vision blocker |
| shutter | `SHUTTER_` | no | no | blast shutter inside a facade bay: open by default; closed it blocks vision and bullets over the whole bay |
| zone_ext | `ZONE_EXT_` | no | no | defender reveal trigger |

**Wall Fortification** (`gadgets/wall-fortification/`) units come in four widths, all 2.0 m high as shipped; on this map scale them to 3.1 m high (y scale 1.55) so they cover the full floor-to-ceiling panel:

| Size | Width | Gadget file | Units on this map |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| small | 1.0 m | `wall-fortification-small` | 4 |
| standard | 1.5 m | `wall-fortification-standard` | 5 |
| large | 3.0 m | `wall-fortification-large` | 12 |
| XL | 4.0 m | `wall-fortification-xl` | 13 |
| hatch | 1.2 × 1.2 m | `wall-fortification-hatch` | 15 hatches |

How the units are placed:

- **Wall units:** a `REINF_` node's origin is the unit origin: bottom centre, on the defender face of the wall.
  Its +Z faces the defender room. Instantiate the gadget as a child at the identity pose, then set the gadget's
  `far_side` node z to `far_side_z` (−0.2 m, the wall thickness) so the star anchors land on the far face.
- **Room for the units:** each unit has at least 0.05 m of hard trim at each end and 0.26 m of clear floor in
  front of it (its deployed depth). Every corner and T-junction is hard for 1.0 m along each arm (and 0.15 m beside every door, window and vault),
  so no unit reaches a corner; there are no pilasters any more.
- **Hatches:** a `HATCH_` node's +Y is the unit's up. Every hatch is reinforced from above, face-up on the slab
  exactly as the gadget ships (anchors 0.3 m down through the slab, terminal ejects onto the floor), as in Siege.
  A site's ceiling hatches (H01/H02 over S4, H07/H08 over S2, H09/H10 over S3) are reinforced by a defender in the
  room above; S1's H14/H15 are in the site floor. `HaneulSurface.farSideZ` on a hatch is the slab depth (0.3,
  informative); `Reinforce()` leaves the hatch unit's own far side as shipped.
- **Budget:** 8 reinforcements per defending team per round. A wall unit of any size and a hatch each count 1
  (`rules.reinforce.budget`).

## 6. Colliders

- **Typed meshes:** the solid typed meshes (`HARD_`, `SOFT_`, `REINF_`, `HATCH_`, `SOFTFLOOR_`, `SOFTCEIL_`,
  `GLASS*`, `RAIL_`, `WALK_`, `LEDGE_`, `BRIDGE_`, `TERRACE`) are built from boxes. Use their own meshes as
  static, non-convex MeshColliders. A wall's hard mesh already has its door and window holes.
- **`COL_` empties:** BoxColliders of size 1 (the node scale is the box size). Stairs collide only on their
  `COL_STAIR_*` boxes (flight ramps, floor and mid landings, the Garden Stair landing, one box per Spiral Stair
  tread); the `STAIR_` meshes are render only. Treat `COL_LD_*` as ladder climb triggers.
- **Plenum:** walls run to the slab underside, so ceilings (`DECO_*_ceiling`) need no collider; a game that wants
  bullets to stop at the ceiling plane can add one from those meshes.
- **Blast shutters:** `SHUTTER_` meshes move (blend shape) and get no collider. Each one's closed state is the box
  `COL_SHUTTER_<bayId>` (the curtain over the bay's whole vision height), which must be enabled only while that shutter
  is closed. `Setup()` adds it disabled; `SetShutter` switches it.
- **No collider:** `DECO_`, `ENV_`, `BACKDROP_city`, `BACKDROP_sky`, `HOLO_stage` (vision only), and every marker.
- **Furniture:** the furniture pack brings its own colliders. The removed layout props have none in the map.

## 6a. Blast shutters

The user asked for blast shutters on every outer room so attackers spawning outside cannot see in through all the
windows, with the shutters left open for now. Design:

- **Where:** every facade bay with glass gets one (166: F1 58, F2 55, F3 53; the two opaque Cold Store bays F1_N04 and
  F1_N05, all spandrel, get none), entry windows included. **No shutter on a wall with a door** (user: "just delete
  the shutter that's on walls with a door"): every bay with a door or portal has no shutter at all, glass beside the
  door included: the Garden Doors F1_S11, the skybridge portal F2_E11, F1_SE00 Bath Door, F1_SW00 Garden side door,
  F1_W08 catwalk door and F3_NW01 gantry door (`rules.shutters.exempt`). Where a neighbour's corner guide would reach
  into a door's clear width at a chamfer corner (F1_E13 beside F1_SE00, F1_S13 beside F1_SW00, F3_N00 beside F3_NW01),
  that curtain end seals in a static steel reveal plate on the door's jamb plane instead (`shutters[].seal` =
  `reveal`, `shutters[].reveal`), never in the doorway. The skybridge tube
  needs none. Double-height bays (Garden, Studio) get a 7.3 m shutter.
- **Inside the glass:** the curtain plane is 0.30–0.37 m inside the glass outer face (`rules.shutters.plane`), behind
  the mullions (0–0.26 m), door frames and barricade slots, so the facade, ledges, fins, reveal zones and rappel are
  unchanged. A facade barricade must stay within `rules.shutters.barricade_clear` (0.16 m) of its slot origin on the
  room side (+Z), or it hits a closed shutter.
- **Housing and guides:** open, the slats stack in a housing in the plenum (bay vision top + 0.8 m, up to the slab),
  out of sight above the ceiling. The guide channels (0.06 m on each side of a mullion line, 0.26–0.40 m deep, floor
  to slab) and housings are hard and static: `HARD_shutters_<F>`. Each mullion line has one small purple status dot at
  2.2 m.
- **Seal:** closed, the curtain covers 0 to the bay's vision height and runs into the housing; each end either sits
  3 cm into its guide channel (and the next bay's guide closes the line, on any floor, unless the line's other side is
  a door bay: then the guide stands alone and carries the line's status dot, `shutters[].guides_solo`) or runs 4 cm into a column or a
  hard wall end that crosses the curtain plane (`shutters[].seal`). Interior glass and balustrades are never used as
  seals. The validator re-checks all of this (every glass bay without a door has one, door bays none, nothing of the shutter system in a doorway, every glass lite covered, full height, seal overlap at the slat depths,
  partner guides over the whole height, no prop in the zone).
- **Mesh and motion:** 14 slats of 0.25 m (34 on double-height bays) overlapping 3 cm, odd slats inset 4 mm (no
  z-fighting where they overlap), a bottom rail with a thin purple LED line on the room side. Every slat moves straight
  down, so the morph blend is exact at every weight and the curtain never opens a gap mid-travel. Travel: 2.0 s for
  the whole height (`travel_s`), eased.
- **Gameplay flags:** indestructible and not reinforceable. Open: no collider, sight and shots pass. Closed: collider
  on, `stopsBullets` and `blocksVision` true.
- **Dressing:** nothing is dressed in the shutter zone. Facade-side ceiling coves now sit 0.64 m inside the glass,
  wall-face panelling and timber rafters stop 0.46 m from the glass, and eight pieces that reached into the zone (a
  laundry duct, the salt-brick ceiling, a garden bed, the Grid steel, the Exec Hall ceiling slats and its warm wall
  light line, the SW corners of the Garden mist sheets, the screening tiers PR73) were trimmed. An in-page ray check
  finds no dressing inside any closed curtain volume; the only overlaps are at the chamfer corners, where the
  neighbouring bay's guide channel crosses the curtain end (that is the corner seal).

`layout.json` `shutters[]`: `{id (= bay id), bay, floor, face, room, site, double_height, covers [lite types], entry,
u0, u1, width (curtain extent along the bay), a, b (plan ends on the curtain plane), plane, seal {a, b: 'guide' or the
column/wall id}, guides, guide_u {a, b: the mullion line of each guided end, along the bay}, guides_solo, door_side
(always null now: door bays get no shutter), span [u0, u1 of the shuttered lites], reveal {a|b: {door_bay, u, s, y, housing_u}} (chamfer ends beside a door), y_closed [0, vision_y1], y_open, travel_m, travel_s, slats, housing {y0, y1, s0, s1, where},
default 'open', node 'SHUTTER_<id>', collider 'COL_SHUTTER_<id>'}`, and `rules.shutters` holds the shared sizes.

Unity (`HaneulTowerMap`):

- `Shutters` (list), `Shutter(bayId)`, `IsShutterClosed(s)`;
- `SetShutter(s, closed, instant = false)`;
- `SetShutters(closed, floor = null, room = null, site = null, instant = false)` returns how many it moved;
  `CloseAllShutters()`, `OpenAllShutters()`, `SetFloorShutters("F2", true)`, `SetRoomShutters("F2_concierge", true)`;
- events `ShutterMoveStarted` / `ShutterMoveFinished` (C#: `(HaneulSurface shutter, bool closed)`) and the
  UnityEvents `onShutterStarted` / `onShutterFinished`;
- `shuttersClosedOnStart` (off: the map starts open) and `shutterTravelOverride`;
- per shutter, `HaneulSurface.shutterClosed`, `shutterT` (0 open .. 1 closed), `shutterTravel`, `shutterCollider`.

The animation runs in `HaneulTowerMap.Update` (`Mathf.MoveTowards`, smoothstep into blend shape weight 0–100). When
opening, the collider goes off at once; when closing, it comes on when the curtain lands. The renderer bounds are
widened to the closed curtain in `Rebuild()` (the imported bounds only hold the open stack, so a closed shutter
could otherwise be culled).

## 7. Unity setup (Unity 2021 LTS to Unity 6, glTFast)

1. Install glTFast (`com.unity.cloud.gltfast`, or `com.atteneder.gltfast` on older versions). Copy
   `unity/Runtime/HaneulTowerMap.cs` and `HaneulSurface.cs` into the project, and `unity/Editor/HaneulTowerMapSetup.cs`
   into an Editor folder.
2. Import `maps/haneul-tower/unity/haneul-tower.glb`, and copy `maps/haneul-tower/layout.json` into the same folder or
   its parent (as a TextAsset).
3. Select the imported `.glb` and run **Tools > Gunmodls > Set Up Haneul Tower Map**. This:
   - adds `HaneulTowerMap` and assigns `layout.json`;
   - runs `Setup()` in the editor;
   - sets static flags by surface type: `HARD_`, slabs, ledges, walkways, terraces, stairs, ladders and the bridge
     structure are batching, occluder and occludee static; `DECO_`/`ENV_` are batching and occludee static (never
     occluders); glass (`GLASS_T_`, `GLASS_B_`, `GLASSI_`, `GLASS_TF_`), rails and `HOLO_` are occludee static only;
     `BACKDROP_city` and `BACKDROP_sky` get no static flags (each is one mesh and one draw call that surrounds the map:
     batching has nothing to merge, it never occludes and occlusion culling cannot hide it);
     destructible types (`SOFT_`, `REINF_`, `HATCH_`, `SOFTFLOOR_`, `SOFTCEIL_`), the moving cradle and the blast
     shutters (`SHUTTER_`, which move) get no static flags, so their meshes stay their own (holes can be cut at runtime) and an occlusion bake never keeps culling
     what is behind a wall after it is blown open;
   - saves `HaneulTowerMap.prefab` next to the `.glb`, with `setupOnAwake` off, so at runtime only the registry is
     rebuilt.

   You can also put `HaneulTowerMap` on a runtime-loaded instance and leave `setupOnAwake` on.
4. Set the layers on the component (soft, hard, trigger, marker) to match your project's physics and bullet
   layers.
5. The blast shutters start open. glTFast imports each `SHUTTER_` mesh with its `closed` blend shape on a
   SkinnedMeshRenderer; call `SetShutters` (§6a) to move them, or play `shutters_close` / `shutters_open`.
6. The cradle: glTFast imports `cradle_cycle` as an animation clip. Play it in a loop on the root (Legacy
   Animation or an Animator). `Setup()` gives the cradle a kinematic Rigidbody so its collider follows. The clip moves the transform, so riders
   are not carried by physics: parent the player to the cradle while standing on it, or apply its per-frame delta.

What `Setup()` does for each node whose name has a known prefix:

- adds `HaneulSurface` with `type` (enum `HaneulSurfaceType`), `id`, `floor`, `destructible`, `reinforceable`,
  `stopsBullets`, `blocksVision` and `walkable`;
- for units and hatches, also sets `fortification` (enum `FortificationSize`), `unitWidth`, `farSideZ`, `wallId`,
  `unitIndex`, `site`, `defenderRoom` and `mountedFromBelow`;
- adds colliders as in §6;
- on `SHUTTER_` nodes (type `Shutter`): no collider; `COL_SHUTTER_<id>` gets a disabled BoxCollider; the registry
  links each shutter to its renderer, blend shape and box, widens the renderer bounds and applies the open state;
- on `BACKDROP_` nodes (type `Backdrop`): no collider, shadow casting and receiving off, light and reflection probes
  off, `allowOcclusionWhenDynamic` off (`HaneulTowerMap.ConfigureBackdrop`);
- sets the layers.

Registry and lookups on `HaneulTowerMap` (also `HaneulTowerMap.Instance`):

- **Lists:**
  - `ReinforceUnits`, `Hatches`
  - `BarricadeDoors`, `BarricadeWindows`
  - `AttackerSpawns`, `DefenderSpawns`
  - `Objectives`, `CoverSpots`, `Callouts`, `PropMarkers`, `RevealZones`, `Shutters`
  - `Sites[id]`, with `objectives`, `defenderSpawns`, `reinforceUnits` and `hatches` per site
- **Surface queries:**
  - `Get(nodeName)`, `Get(collider)`, `SurfaceOf(collider)`
  - `IsDestructible(collider)`, `IsReinforceable(collider)`, `StopsBullets(collider)`
- **Gadget sizes:**
  - `FortificationFor(collider)`
  - `WidthOf(size)`, `GadgetFile(size)`
  - `SizeOf("xl")`, `SizeOfWidth(width)`
- **Reinforcing:**
  - `NearestReinforcePoint(point, maxDistance)`
  - `Reinforce(surface, gadgetPrefab)`: marks the surface reinforced, instantiates the gadget on its mount and sets
    `far_side`. Play the gadget's `deploy` clip yourself.
- **Places:**
  - `AttackerSpawnPoints("A1")`, `GetSite("S2")`
  - `Callout(roomId)`, `RoomName(roomId)`, `FloorAt(worldPoint)`
  - `ReinforceBudget`
  - `FromLayout(x, y, z)`

Destruction itself (holes in `REINF_` panels, glass breaking, hatch breaching) is left to the game. Each wall's `reinforce.panels`
(u0, u1, 0–3.1 m) are its only breakable parts; `walls[].openings` and the rest of the wall are hard.

Materials:

- **Emissive strength:** older glTFast versions ignore `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` (recent 5.x/6.x versions
  read it; check yours before baking), so the export bakes strengths up to 1 into `emissiveFactor`. Brighter
  emissives keep the extension; multiply their emission by
  `extensions.KHR_materials_emissive_strength.emissiveStrength` in your shader or post-import step if you want the
  same bloom. `backdrop_city` is one of them (strength 3.2: its emissive atlas stores linear emission / 3.2, so the
  beacons and light strips keep their HDR range); without the extension the city reads about 3× too dim.
- **Textures:** every material has real albedo, normal and ORM maps (no shader-only effects), except the baked
  backdrop atlas, which has baseColor, emissive and metallic-roughness only.
- **Not exported:** rain, lightning, moving traffic, the light-rail train, beacon blink, screen flicker and the soft
  atmosphere layers (ridge mist, rain haze rings, the broadcast tower halo) are viewer-only. Recreate them in the
  engine (fog covers most of the haze).

## 8. Editing the layout and rebuilding

`layout.json` comes from the tables in `tools/build_layout.py`: rooms, walls, openings, reinforce units, hatches,
facade bays, exterior, spawns, sites, props and routes. The generator also clips walls at columns, finds each
reinforceable wall's clear face and fits its units.

```
cd maps/haneul-tower
python3 tools/build_layout.py            # tables -> layout.json  (-v prints the reinforcement fit report)
python3 tools/validate_layout.py         # must say 0 errors (24 warnings are known: 23 door chains of 30-40 m, Booth 2); also checks the shutters
python3 tools/render_plans.py            # -> floorplans/ (layout.json and floorplans/ are the defaults)
python3 tools/navtime.py                 # spawn -> site and rotation timings
python3 tools/build_viewer.py            # src/ + layout.json -> viewer.html   (--fast for a quick page)
cd ../..
node tools/export-glb.js maps/haneul-tower/viewer.html          # -> haneul-tower.glb (about 1 min 45 s with page load on swiftshader)
node tools/unity-export-map.js maps/haneul-tower/viewer.html    # -> unity/haneul-tower.glb
node tools/render-preview.js maps/haneul-tower/viewer.html      # -> preview.png (see the note below)
node tools/check-glb.js maps/haneul-tower/haneul-tower.glb       # node and material report
```

Validate with the Khronos glTF validator (npm `gltf-validator`, `validateBytes`). The target is 0 errors. The current
files give 0 errors; both glbs have 2,486 warnings, all `MESH_PRIMITIVE_GENERATED_TANGENT_SPACE`, and 494 infos
(485 `UNUSED_OBJECT`, 9 `IMAGE_NPOT_DIMENSIONS`; the extra 169 are the unused UVs of the untextured LED primitives on the
shutters and housings).

Notes on the tools under swiftshader (software GL): `render-preview.js` can hit Playwright's default 30 s screenshot
timeout on this map; the shipped `preview.png` was taken by calling `window.__model.preview()` and screenshotting at
1600×900 with a 240 s timeout. `check-glb.js --render` frames the whole scene, which for a map is the sky dome, so it
is only useful for its node and material report here.

Rules for edits:

- **Unit sizes:** reinforceable walls need a clear face that fits whole units (1.0, 1.5, 3.0 and 4.0 m) with
  ≥ 0.05 m trim at each end. The generator places them, and the validator checks them against walls, columns,
  props and the other units.
- **Column lines:** a wall that meets the glass on a column line stops at the column face, and the strip to the
  glass becomes a hard column wrap.
- **Clearances:** keep 1.0 m on each side of interior openings, 1.5 m inside facade entries, 0.7 m round hatches
  and 1.5 m beyond stair ends.
- **No text:** no logos and no readable text anywhere. Abstract screen graphics only.

The viewer's engine reads `layout.json` and builds everything by surface type, so structural edits need no code
changes. Dressing in `src/parts/*.js` is placed by room and wall id, so check it after you move rooms.
`floor_1.js` to `floor_3.js` were generated from per-floor source snippets that are not shipped, so edit the
generated files directly.

Each part file defines one `PARTS.<name> = function (K) {...}`. It runs in a try/catch: a failure is undone and
logged to `window.__partErrors`, and the blockout stays.

The page's debug contract is `window.__model = {id, exportScenes, exportGLB, preview, ready, debug}`. `debug`
offers:

- `set(view)` and `floor(n)`;
- `overlay(on)`, `labels`, `markers`, `rain`, `skyline`;
- `only([layers])`, `clip(box)`;
- `eye(pos, yaw, pitch)`, `cradle(t)`, `stats()`, `partErrors()`, `renderOnce()`;
- `shutters(closed, instant)` and `shutterWeight()` (0 open .. 1 closed). In the UI: the **Shutters** button or B.

`exportGLB({ unity: true })` is the Unity variant (the same nodes; the backdrop is never tiled).

## 9. Known limitations

- **Reinforcement budget:** with every breakable wall reinforceable, each site has 10–14 candidates (S1 11, S2 12,
  S3 10, S4 14: wall units plus the two ceiling hatches) against a budget of 8. Units on non-site walls have
  `defender_side: either` (no site side); they are placed on the side_neg face.
- **Backdrop bake (user rule: one mesh, one material):** `core.js` `buildBackdrop()` bakes it when the page loads
  (about 4 s of the build). Tiled textures get a region of whole repeats in the atlas (facades 2×4 tiles at 384 px per
  24 m tile, about 6 cm per texel; roofs 6×6 tiles; apartment walls 2×6), and each face is shifted by whole repeats
  and only split where it spans more than its region, which takes the city from 107k to 132k triangles. Fine tiles
  (louvres, water ripples) and untextured materials are flat swatches. Per-material environment strength, haze strength
  and beacon blink are viewer-only (the occlusion channel and a vertex attribute); a game lights the backdrop with its
  emissive map and its own fog.
- **Furniture:** the map has no furniture. The cover and sightline breakers that the design relies on (for example
  the Veranda lantern PR15) exist only as `PROP_` markers until furniture-pack pieces are placed on them. Keep
  their `box` and `h` when `breaks_sightline` is true.
- **Size:** the export is large (41.6 MB; the 166 shutters add 3.3 MB, mostly their closed morph positions; shutters, guides, housings and reveal plates are 31.3k triangles). About 65 % of the triangles (132k in `BACKDROP_city`, 4.6k in
  `BACKDROP_sky`, plus dressing) are scenery; a game can also replace the two backdrop nodes with its own skybox.
- **Destruction:** each `REINF_` unit is one panel mesh; there is no pre-fractured geometry.
- **Collider cooking:** a glTFast mesh that is not CPU-readable cannot be cooked into a MeshCollider at runtime, so
  `Setup()` falls back to the mesh's box. Run the editor setup, which cooks at build time.
- **Extras:** Unity cannot read them by default. Everything the runtime needs comes from names plus `layout.json`.
- **Blast shutters:** they are one rigid design for every bay (no per-room variants) and indestructible. Closed, they
  also block the defenders' own view out and every facade entry, so a mode that closes them needs its own rule for when
  they open. Their collider is a box that appears only when fully closed (a player under a closing shutter is not
  pushed out). The morph blend has no separate normals, which is exact here (pure translations). The facade
  barricades must stay within 0.16 m of their slot (§6a).
