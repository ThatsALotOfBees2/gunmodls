# Calamity-spirit style (original designs only)

Full research-based guide with tables, palettes, per-class shapes, addon flavours, VFX recipe and
concept seeds: `STYLE_GUIDE.md` in this folder. Read the sections you need before designing.

## Hard limits
- Never recreate a specific existing item, boss or character (shape, name, sprite, signature
  design), even renamed or called fan art. Borrow general ideas only: palettes as broad families,
  motifs (feathers, flames, crystal, cosmic voids), mechanics, how effects layer and time.
- No copied text, logos or sprite art. Give everything our own name and details.

## Core DNA (short)
1. Silhouette first: one bold asymmetric shape; big head (60-75% of length), thin dark grip.
2. Built core + living overgrowth (feathers, flame, crystal, horns, goo) on a manufactured part.
3. Dark body, bright accents; glow only on gems, seams, edges, veins; one near-white hottest spot.
4. Hue-shifted shading: shadows toward blue/purple, highlights toward yellow/cyan; dark-hue outlines.
5. Tier is visible: size, glow area, hue count and effect layers rise with progression
   (natural -> elemental -> holy/solar -> cosmic -> regal phoenix -> prismatic tech -> ash/ember final,
   where the final tier pulls glow back for menace).
6. One palette ramp per theme (2-3 hues + black/white, dark -> saturated -> near-white).
7. Effects in layers: crisp object + soft additive glow + short streaks/sparks (+ screen effect at peaks).
8. Telegraph -> strike -> linger; bigger threat, longer warning. Screen shake/flash/distortion are brief
   punctuation centred on impacts.

## Translating pixel art to 3D
Keep: silhouette, palette ramp, glow placement, motif rhythm. Reinterpret: outlines become rim light
and bevel highlights; hue-shift becomes tinted shadow/emissive gradients; pixel clusters become real
construction (layers, facets, bevels); bloom comes from emissive parts (separate Neon parts in Roblox,
emissive maps in Unity).

## Use with the other skills
For gunmodls assets also follow `siege-house-style` (no text, delivery checklist, pipelines); a
Calamity-style piece may break the purple-only palette when the user asks for it, but keeps every
other house rule.
